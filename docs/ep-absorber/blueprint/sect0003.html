3 Share of the Bode–Fano ceiling

Theorem 3.1
#

(Bode–Fano ceiling.) Every passive absorber with a rational scattering matrix, lossless background and intrinsic loss \(L\) has \(B\le 4\pi L/\ln 10\).

Proof ▶

Rank-one reduction, \(\sigma _{\min }(S)\ge |\det S|\) and Jensen’s formula give \(\int _\mathbb {R}\ln |1/\det S|\, d\omega \le 2\pi L\); a 90% band contributes at least \(\tfrac 12\ln 10\) per unit frequency. Not yet formalized.
