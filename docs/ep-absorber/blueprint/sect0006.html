6 The sharp ceiling for every order

For every \(N\ge 2\), \(\sup B_{\mathrm{EP}}=2\sqrt{2/3}\, L\approx 1.633\, L\), attained at \(N=2\).

Proof ▶

Attainment is Theorem 2.6. The upper bound uses a Schwarz-chain representation of Hurwitz polynomials, convexity along adjacent chain couplings, an entropy dual for each multiplicity of the coalesced zero, a second-order expansion around the dimer and 984 cells checked in 160-bit interval arithmetic (Supplemental Material, Secs. S5, S6 and S16). Not yet formalized.
