5 The EP envelope and the one-sided reach

Definition 5.1
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • EP.Jθ
  • EP.Mfac

\(J_\theta =\begin{pmatrix} 1 & e^{i\theta } \\ -e^{-i\theta } & -1 \end{pmatrix}\) and the elementary transfer step \(M_j(\omega )=I+\frac{2i\gamma _j}{\omega }J_{\theta _j}\), with loss weights \(\gamma _j\ge 0\), \(\sum _j\gamma _j=L\) and phases \(\theta _j\). In the canonical form of a full-order EP, \(P(\omega )/\omega ^N=\big(\prod _j M_j(\omega )\big)_{11}\).

Theorem 5.2
#

Uses

  • Definition 5.1

(Canonical form.) Every perfectly absorbing full-order EP, with any number of ports and any loss matrix, reduces to the canonical form of Definition 5.1 without changing \(L\).

Proof ▶

Rank-one reduction by Darlington synthesis, a unitary Schur transformation making the zero operator strictly upper triangular, and a diagonal gauge. Not yet formalized.

Lemma 5.3
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 5.1

Lean declarations

  • EP.envelope_prod

For real \(\omega \ne 0\), \(\big|\big(\prod _j M_j(\omega )\big)_{11}\big|\le \cosh \sum _j\operatorname {asinh}(2\gamma _j/|\omega |)\).

Proof ▶

Each \(M_j\) has diagonal entries of modulus \(\cosh \rho _j\) and off-diagonal entries of modulus \(\sinh \rho _j\), \(\rho _j=\operatorname {asinh}(2\gamma _j/|\omega |)\); entry bounds of this form add under multiplication by \(\cosh (a+b)=\cosh a\cosh b+\sinh a\sinh b\).

Lemma 5.4
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • EP.envelope_jensen

\(\sum _j\operatorname {asinh}(2\gamma _j/|\omega |)\le N\operatorname {asinh}\big(2L/(N|\omega |)\big)\).

Proof ▶

\(\operatorname {asinh}\) is concave on \([0,\infty )\) (its derivative \((1+x^2)^{-1/2}\) is decreasing); apply Jensen’s inequality with equal weights.

Theorem 5.5
✓

Lean declarations

  • EP.reach

If \(|r(\omega )|^2\le 1/10\), i.e. \(\big|\big(\prod _jM_j\big)_{11}\big|\ge \sqrt{10}=\cosh (\operatorname {asinh}3)\), then \(|\omega |\le X_N=2L/\big(N\sinh (\operatorname {asinh}3/N)\big)\).

Proof ▶

Combine the two lemmas and invert \(\cosh \) and \(\sinh \), both increasing on \([0,\infty )\).

Lemma 5.6
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • EP.reach_lt

For every \(N\ge 1\), \(X_N{\lt}2L/\operatorname {asinh}3\approx 1.100\, L\).

Proof ▶

\(N\sinh (\kappa /N){\gt}\kappa \) because \(\sinh x{\gt}x\) for \(x{\gt}0\).
